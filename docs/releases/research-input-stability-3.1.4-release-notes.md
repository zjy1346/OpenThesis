## What's Changed

### English

- Across the previously unpublished 3.1.0–3.1.4 work, improved preservation and recovery of useful research content, with isolated issues no longer unnecessarily blocking unrelated analysis.
- Strengthened financial research from source intake through reporting: retained reliable partial facts, improved evidence lineage and ROE row/column mapping, and matched interim comparisons to the correct metric periods.

- Refined desktop and exported report presentation so financial periods, localized gaps, model activity, and research status are easier to follow.
